## B. Score normalization and oracle separation — PASS

The released evaluator macro-averages query metrics, with binary qrels determining ideal DCG and the available relevant-document count. Precision uses its cutoff. None of the ordinary metric denominators is a model score statistic. In BGE, unit-vector normalization and cosine similarity are part of retrieval. The numerical parity reporters use the ordinary `NDCG@10` key. The independent verifier calculated all 400 ordinary and 96 diagnostic run-level values from saved scores and source positives within half a unit at five decimal places. See `vendor/Tempo/retrievers.py:294–318,1108–1132`, `scripts/report_tempo_parity.py:8–21`, `scripts/report_tempo_retrievers.py:12–27`, `reports/review_tools/verify_tempo_results.py:106–124,202–219`, and `reports/review_tools/tempo_snapshot_evidence.json:23–29`.

The six gold-assisted oracle values can credit a relevant document outside the original top 100. The evaluator assigns zero to all other returned items and sends the whole result map to trec evaluation, where the zero scores tie. This is released diagnostic behavior. The smoke fixture demonstrates the boundary case. No deployed or candidate-constrained ceiling claim follows. See `vendor/Tempo/retrievers.py:1134–1169` and `reports/tempo_smoke_review.md:27–31`.
